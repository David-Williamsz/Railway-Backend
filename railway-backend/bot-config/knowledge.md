## How working together actually works

1. Understand — what's actually slowing the business down
2. Design — smallest useful version of a fix
3. Build — the actual thing
4. Connect — wiring it into what the business already uses
5. Automate — removing the manual step once the first version proves itself
6. Improve — adjusting based on real usage

No self-serve checkout. The process is: talk → agree scope and price with
Michael directly → Michael sends a payment link once agreed. The bot
never processes payment or names a final number.
